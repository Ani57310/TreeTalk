# Calophyllum inophyllum

**Source:** https://www.ifgtbtreegenie.in/post/calophyllum-inophyllum

## Overview

Calophyllum is a tree-borne oil seed species. Its carpel contains about 50% oil.

The oil is used for:
- Biodiesel production
- Treatment of skin diseases
- Soap manufacturing

Medicinal-grade pure oil commands a high market price. The oil cake is used as manure. The tree is also planted as a wind barrier along seashores, as a roadside shade tree, and as an ornamental tree. It is suitable for intercropping with coconut plantations.

The tree starts yielding after approximately **6 years**.

---

# Cultivation Method

## Soil Type

- Suitable for sandy soil.
- Can also grow well in clayey, lime, and gravelly soils.

## Spacing

- **4 m × 4 m**

## Pit Size

- **2 cubic feet**

## Irrigation

- Irrigate **3–4 times per month** during the first two years.
- Drip irrigation is recommended.

## Manuring

After three months of planting, apply either:

- 250 g Neem cake per plant, or
- 500 g Farm Yard Manure (FYM) per plant.

## Weed Management

- Remove weeds once every six months.
- After two years, prune branches to improve yield.

---

# Oil Extraction

Kernels should be dried under shade for one week until they reach approximately **5% moisture content**.

Oil can then be extracted using:

- Expeller
- Oil Ghani

# Yield

A **10-year-old tree** produces approximately:

- 12 kg fruits
- 3 kg kernels
- 2.1 litres oil

At **4 m × 4 m spacing**, approximately **35 trees** can be planted in a **0.25-acre plot**, producing around **75 litres of Calophyllum oil**.

Clones begin fruiting after **4–5 years**.

## Fruit Yield by Tree Age

| Tree Age | Fruit Yield |
|-----------|-------------|
| 4–5 years | 4–6 kg |
| 10 years | 10–60 kg |

# Seed Handling Techniques

## Seed Collection

- July–August
- December

## Seed Treatment

Removing the leathery fruit coat improves germination approximately **20-fold**, resulting in **95–100% germination**.

## Seedling Production

- Sow seeds at **1 cm depth**.
- Use **13 cm × 25 cm** polythene bags.
- Fill bags with:
  - 2 parts red earth
  - 1 part sand
  - 1 part farmyard manure
- Irrigate twice daily.
- Keep seedlings under shade.
- Harden seedlings in sunlight for 10 days before field planting.

## Seed Drying

Dry under shade only. Excessive drying reduces seed viability.

## Seed Storage

- Remove the seed coat.
- Store seeds in an earthen pot.
- Cover with cotton cloth.
- Place the pot inside a bucket filled with wet sand.

This method maintains optimum moisture and preserves viability for **4–6 months**, with **95% germination**.

---

# Oil Extraction from Seed

Approximately **1–1.5 litres** of Calophyllum oil can be extracted from **15 kg of dry kernels**.

Sugar molasses should be added during extraction in an oil mill or ghani.

The remaining oil cake can be used as organic manure.
